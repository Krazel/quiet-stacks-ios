# Royal succession

The player hopes to succeed Master Alden, the ageing Royal Librarian. A letter
introduces his final test: return every volume to its rightful place. Once all
960 volumes occupy their exact slots, Alden passes on the brass keys and the
player becomes Royal Librarian. The ending follows the final placement effect.

The opening is remembered, and saved books remain intact. Demo offers Read the
letter and Preview ending without awarding completion. All text stays in English.
Both scenes use a warm, gold-edged letter panel matching the library.

Books now remain visible as they cross the lower edge of the screen. Dropping
books in the home-gesture area remains restricted. Panning reaches both complete
painted walls at every zoom without changing the room artwork or shelf geometry.

Verification covers starting, continuing, exact completion, interrupted
completion, previews, save restoration, small screens and both renderers.
